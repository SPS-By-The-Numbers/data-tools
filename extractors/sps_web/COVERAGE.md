# Board-contracts pipeline — coverage, gaps, and why

Per-school-year counts for the `extractors/sps_web/` board-contracts
pipeline (see `PLAN.md` for the design, `docs/guides/BOARD_CONTRACTS.md` for
the consumer-facing data guide). The crawl (Phase B), extraction/linking
pipeline (Phases C-F), and the 2016+ Board Action Report detail pass
(Phase E3, `bar_fill.py`) are complete; `publish.py` (G1) has produced the
final spreadsheet in `out_sps_web/publish/` (BigQuery load still pending —
owner's call). Every table here is generated by a specific module — rerun
that module and re-copy the table to refresh a number; nothing here is
hand-maintained.

`qa/reconcile_report.md` check 6 confirms no school year's action-row or
admitted-item count drops more than 50% versus both neighboring years —
2013-14 through 2015-16 (the years that were empty while the Blackboard
crawl was still pending) are now fully populated and consistent with their
neighbors. Board-action rows are usually below admitted contract-like items
for a healthy year — an introduction and its action-meeting vote fold into
one row (see the guide's §5), so pairing *reduces* the row count relative
to `extracted.jsonl`'s one-row-per-item shape — except where a year has
several multi-vendor motions (§6 of the guide), whose extra member rows can
push the count back up (2022-23 is the clearest example: 75 action rows
against 69 admitted items). Overall pairing rate is 83.2% (969/1,165
introductions, `qa/link_report.md`); see that report's per-year table for
the exact rate by year.

### Meetings with neither an agenda nor minutes (inventory-level)

111 of 1,023 meetings (11%) have no agenda and no minutes *document* at all
by A1/A2's cheap `kind_guess` — the full per-year breakdown and row-by-row
list is in `qa/coverage_matrix.md`. Of those, 30 have **zero** documents of
any kind: 13 are WP posts that link nothing (cancelled or video-only
meetings) and 17 are Wayback-only dates whose every file deduped onto a
different meeting's date (a byproduct of A2 taking the date from the
filename while the file physically sat in a different meeting's folder) —
treat that second group as "meeting happened, documents filed elsewhere,"
not a real coverage hole. **The tighter, final number is reconcile check
1's 26 meetings** (table above) — it reads actual classified `kind`, not
the filename-guess `kind_guess`, so it's the number to trust for "does this
meeting have a usable primary source."

### Previous years (2004-05 → 2015-16): why amounts stay blank

Measured 2026-08-25 after extending the BAR pass to all years. Pre-2016
there are 1,085 action rows; only about half carry an amount and two thirds a
vendor, and **the missing figures are not in any archived document**:

- Zero of the pre-2012 rows with a blank amount have a dollar figure anywhere
  in their item text — the extractor is not missing printed money. The
  legacy/archive agendas are 1–4 page item lists and the 2011-12 → 2013-14
  Blackboard minutes are terse ("execute a contract with Verizon Wireless").
- The documents that state fiscal impact — Board Action Reports — survive as
  separate files only for 2015-16 (207 with a fiscal-impact section) and a
  few dozen from 2004-05 → 2010-11 (`*actionreport.pdf` attachments the
  Wayback Machine happened to capture). The 2011-12 → 2014-15 Blackboard
  meeting folders never contained them.
- Extending `bar_fill.py` to all years therefore added 18 amounts (2015-16
  went from 105 to 123 of 163 rows) plus a handful earlier; pre-2016 now
  has 560 of 1,085 action rows with an amount and 730 with a vendor.

The way to fill these years is the source, not the code: the SPS Archives
hold the printed **agenda packets for 2006–2012** (ArchivesSpace record
GS505A13-R, "includes digital index", 206-252-0796 / archives@seattleschools.org),
and the Board Office keeps action reports for later years. The request list
is generated by the pipeline: `out_sps_web/qa/missing_amounts_pre2016.csv`
— one row per pre-2016 contract action with no amount (date, item number,
action type, vendor, title, contract id, citation), 437 items across 169
meetings as of 2026-08-25. If those packets arrive as PDFs, drop them under
`out_sps_web/raw/<era>/<date>/`, run `totext` + `classify` (they classify as
`bar`), and rerun `bar_fill` — nothing else changes.
