# Board-contracts pipeline — coverage, gaps, and why

Per-school-year counts for the `extractors/sps_web/` board-contracts
pipeline (see `PLAN.md` for the design, `docs/guides/BOARD_CONTRACTS.md` for
the consumer-facing data guide). The crawl (Phase B) and extraction/linking
pipeline (Phases C–F) are complete; `publish.py` (G1) has produced the
final spreadsheet in `out_sps_web/publish/` (BigQuery load still pending —
owner's call). Every table here is generated by a specific module — rerun
that module and re-copy the table to refresh a number; nothing here is
hand-maintained.

## 5. Why documents are missing, by category

Regenerated by: `inventory_merge.py` (`qa/coverage_matrix.md`'s "Gaps" and
"Left aside" sections), `fetch.py`'s failure log
(`manifest/fetch_failures.jsonl`), and `reconcile.py` (`qa/reconcile_report.md`
check 1).

| Category | Count | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| **spsarchivepublic outage** | n/a (0 live) | The ArchivesSpace-indexed file server (`spsarchivepublic.seattleschools.org`) answers with a TCP reset for both `curl` and Chrome — confirmed dead at scouting time in `PLAN.md` §1 and never came back during the crawl. All 222 "edited agenda" documents for 2006-2012 were recovered **only** via Wayback CDX (`archive` era), 227 raw files fetched. |
| **Thin 2011-12** | 55 meetings, 15 with minutes | The single worst-covered year with any real WP/Blackboard presence: the Blackboard-era inventory holds only 41 documents for 2011-12 vs. 171 for 2012-13 and 178 for 2013-14 (`manifest/wayback_inventory_report.md`'s blackboard section) — the site's early files are simply thinner than later years, not a crawl gap. The `archive` supplement fills some of the difference. |
| **Blackboard era, now fully crawled** | 1,524 documents in the manifest | 1,395 fetched (91.5%); 129 have no usable Wayback capture at any timestamp and are considered permanently missing, not pending. |
| **Legacy era, now fully crawled** | 845 documents in the manifest | 204 fetched (24% of the 866 pre-dedupe rows the crawl ran over: 204 + 654 missing + 8 corrupt = 866); 654 have no usable Wayback capture, and a further 8 (2008 presentation PDFs) have a capture that exists but is corrupt — every attempt truncates at ~130 KB regardless of retry. Legacy's raw fetch rate is genuinely low: the CDX index for `/area/board/*` returns a 404/301/302/no-capture status for the large majority of its URLs (see `manifest/wayback_inventory_report.md`'s legacy CDX status distribution), not a symptom of an incomplete run. |
| **SharePoint login-walled documents** | 3 (6 retry attempts logged) | 2021-22+ SharePoint share tokens whose `download.aspx` request comes back as an HTML login page instead of PDF bytes on every retry (`fetch_failures.jsonl`, `login_wall`). Permanently unreachable by the anonymous-guest recipe this pipeline uses. |
| **Committee packets set aside** | 1,333 (`manifest/wayback_committees.jsonl`) | A&F / Operations / Curriculum committee packets, flagged but not merged into the meeting/document inventory. Owner decision on whether to include them is still open (`PLAN.md` §4) — out of scope for this dataset as it stands. |
| **Undated Wayback rows** | 139 (`manifest/wayback_undated.jsonl`) | No meeting date could be derived from the folder or filename (year-level dockets, loose files at a folder root). Not merged into `meetings.jsonl`/`documents.jsonl` for the same reason a citation needs a meeting date to be useful. |
| **Links with no fetch recipe at all** | 575 (`manifest/documents_nofetch.jsonl`) | Breakdown: `html_page_not_a_document` 263, `video_link_not_a_document` 150, `office_forms_link_not_a_document` 88, `non_document_link` 67, `sharepoint_link_without_share_token` 5, `malformed_source_url` 2. |
| **Meetings with documents but no usable agenda/minutes text** | 26 (2.5% of 1,023) | `reconcile.py` check 1 (final, post-crawl figure): these meetings have fetched, classified documents — but every one of them is a BAR, presentation, or packet, never an agenda or minutes. A structural gap in what the district ever published for those meetings, not a crawl or classification miss. |
| **Pre-2004-05** | out of scope | WA State Digital Archives title 551 is not online ("coming soon"); anything earlier is a manual request to SPS Archives (archives@seattleschools.org, 206-252-0796). Not automatable — noted, not attempted. |

### Meetings with neither an agenda nor minutes (inventory-level)

111 of 1,023 meetings (11%) have no agenda and no minutes *document* at all
by A1/A2's cheap `kind_guess` — the full per-year breakdown and row-by-row
list is in `qa/coverage_matrix.md`. Of those, 30 have **zero** documents of
any kind: 13 are WP posts that link nothing (cancelled or video-only
meetings) and 17 are Wayback-only dates whose every file deduped onto a
different meeting's date (a byproduct of A2 taking the date from the
filename while the file physically sat in a different meeting's folder) —
treat that second group as "meeting happened, documents filed elsewhere,"
not a real coverage hole. **The tighter, final number is reconcile check
1's 26 meetings** (table above) — it reads actual classified `kind`, not
the filename-guess `kind_guess`, so it's the number to trust for "does this
meeting have a usable primary source."
